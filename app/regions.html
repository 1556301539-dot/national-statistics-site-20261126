For statistics Iceland is cut into eight regions — a division fixed in its current form in December 2020. One of them holds nearly two-thirds of the country. Two of them hold about 7,000 people each. The bars below are the whole country, region by region.

The odd one out is the Westfjords. 7,168 people on 9,409 km² — 0.8 per km² — and the only region whose population fell over the long run before stabilising. Its largest town, Ísafjörður, has about 2,600 people. Source: Statistics Iceland, 2024.
