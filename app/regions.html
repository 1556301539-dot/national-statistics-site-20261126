Regions

Japan's 47 prefectures range from Tokyo, with 14.25 million people on 2,200 km², to Hokkaido, with 4.99 million people on 83,422 km². The ten largest prefectures hold about 58.6% of the national population. Only Tokyo and Okinawa grew between the 2020 and 2025 censuses; the other 45 prefectures shrank.
