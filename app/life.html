A year in Iceland is a year of light and weather swinging to extremes: 21 hours of daylight in June, four in December, and a Gulf Stream climate that keeps Reykjavík warmer in January than New York. This page is the practical half of the ledger — what the sky does, what things cost, and the facts that frame the country.
