Economy

Japan is the world's fourth-largest economy by nominal GDP. In yen terms the economy keeps growing; in dollar terms it shrank after 2021 as the yen weakened — the same economy, two different lenses.

Nominal GDP

Yen: Cabinet Office, via FocusEconomics (revised figures), billion yen. Dollars: World Bank, World Development Indicators (current US dollars), billion dollars. The World Bank 2024 figure (US$4.03 trillion) differs from the IMF/Cabinet Office figure (US$4.19 trillion) by vintage and method; the IMF figure is used as the headline. Nominal GDP per capita in 2024 was US$33,785 (Cabinet Office, Dec 2025; IMF: US$33,820), ranking 24th among OECD members — a record low rank.

Real GDP growth

Source: IMF, World Economic Outlook, April 2026. Percent change per year. The Cabinet Office's first estimate put 2024 real growth at +0.1% (Feb 2025 release); the IMF figure is shown here, by vintage and revision.

Source: World Bank, national accounts, 2023. A 2024 vintage gives about 1.0% agriculture, 26.8% industry and 71.4% services (World Bank national accounts via economicactivity.org).

Manufacturing is the largest part of industry; the automotive industry alone generates roughly one-fifth of Japan's total manufacturing value added (World Bank / GDPIndex comparison, 2024).

Exports and imports. Exports of goods in 2024 were 107.09 trillion yen and imports 112.72 trillion yen (Ministry of Finance, Trade Statistics of Japan). Exports of goods and services were US$921.0 billion and imports US$958.4 billion (World Bank). See the Trade page for the full series.
