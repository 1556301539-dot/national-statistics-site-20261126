Iceland sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, and it shows in the power grid: rivers and volcanoes make the electricity, volcanoes heat the homes, and the country uses more electricity per person than anywhere else on Earth — mostly to smelt aluminium.

Where the electricity comes from, 2024

Hydro does the heavy lifting; geothermal does the rest. Fossil fuel is a rounding error.

Source: Eurostat, electricity generation by source, 2024. The renewable share has been above 99.8% in every year since 1990.

All energy, not just electricity

Electricity is the clean part. Counting heat and transport too, geothermal still dominates — it is the largest single energy source in the country — but oil keeps a tenth of the total, almost all of it in ships, aircraft, and cars.
