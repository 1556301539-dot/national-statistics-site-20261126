Iceland is the smallest country in this ledger's league and the strangest: 387,000 people on a volcanic island in the North Atlantic, running an almost entirely renewable grid, catching a million tonnes of fish, and hosting nearly six times their own number in visitors every year. This site puts the whole country in one place, figure by figure, with the source and year under each one.
