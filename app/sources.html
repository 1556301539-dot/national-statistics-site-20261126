Sources & method

How this atlas was put together, and where every number comes from.

What this site is

Japan Statistical Atlas is an independent compilation of Japan's official statistics. It gathers figures published by Japanese government agencies and international organisations into one readable atlas. It is not affiliated with the Government of Japan, the Statistics Bureau of Japan, or any other government body, and it is not an official publication.

The site presents Japan's national statistics for a general reader: the population, the economy, labour, prices, trade and the regions. Every figure on every page carries its source and reference year next to it, so any number can be traced back to the publication it came from.

How figures were compiled

Figures were collected on 26 November 2026 from official and international sources. Where two reputable sources disagree — for example the World Bank and IMF figures for 2024 nominal GDP in US dollars, or the IMF and Cabinet Office figures for 2024 real GDP growth — the atlas shows one figure as the headline and names the other, with the reason for the difference (vintage, revision or method), rather than silently picking one. Where a figure could not be verified from a reputable source, it was left out entirely.

Within any single chart, one series is used consistently. Derived figures, such as population density computed from a census population and a published area, are labelled as computed. Prefectural populations in the Regions table are 2025 census preliminary counts, except for the three smallest prefectures, which are 2024 estimates and are labelled as such.

Charts on this site are drawn by hand-written code from the cited figures — no chart is a placeholder and no figure is invented. The site loads no external resources: no web fonts, no scripts, no images and no tracking.

Update note

Figures are as published by the sources above at the time of compilation (26 November 2026). Statistical agencies revise their estimates: the Cabinet Office, for example, revised its first estimate of 2024 nominal GDP upward after publication. Where a revision is known, the atlas uses the revised figure and says so. New census counts, vital statistics and national accounts will be folded in as they are released.

Disclaimer

Figures are reproduced from the cited publications; the atlas adds presentation, comparison and commentary, not new measurement. Figures in US dollars depend on exchange rates and on the source's conversion method, so dollar figures from different publishers are not directly comparable. This site is an independent compilation and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of Japan or any statistical agency.
