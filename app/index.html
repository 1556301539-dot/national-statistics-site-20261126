Society in brief

Life expectancy at birth in 2024 was 84.04 years overall — 81.09 for men and 87.13 for women (MHLW, Simplified Life Tables 2024). In 2019 men averaged 72.68 healthy years and women 75.38 healthy years (MHLW).

There were 80.58 centenarians per 100,000 people nationally in 2025; Shimane Prefecture leads at 168.69 per 100,000, for the 13th consecutive year (MHLW).

More than 1 in 10 people in Japan is now aged 80 or older (World Economic Forum, citing Japanese government statistics).

The population is projected to fall to roughly 87 million by 2070, with about 40% aged 65 or over (National Institute of Population and Social Security Research).
